### System Features

Features of Catan
- F1: Ability to display the game board, with hexagonal tiles, resource types, number tokens, and ports 
- F2: Ability to create players with their respective colors, resources, and buildings at the beginning of the game 
- F3: Ability to place initial settlements and roads during the setup phase according to setup rules 
- F4: Ability to count the number of turns taken, as well as have functional turn-based gameplay 
- F5: Ability to roll dice and distribute resources to players based on settlements and cities adjacent to matching number tokens, excluding robber-blocked hexes 
- F6: Ability for players to build settlements, cities, and roads on valid locations according to game rules, deducting the appropriate resources 
- F7: Ability for players to trade resources with the bank, ports, and other players during their turn 
- F8: Ability to move the robber to a new hex when a 7 is rolled or a Knight card is played, blocking resource production and allowing the active player to steal from an adjacent player 
- F9: Ability for players to draw, hold, and play development cards including Knights, Victory Points, Road Building, Year of Plenty, and Monopoly 
- F10: Ability to track and award Longest Road and Largest Army special cards to qualifying players, transferring them when another player qualifies 
- F11: Ability to detect when a player wins the game by reaching 10 or more victory points on their turn
